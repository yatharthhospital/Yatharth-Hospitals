BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

Plan Ahead for a More Organised Visit

A little preparation helps patients and attendants make better use of consultation time. Keep important documents together and confirm your visit details before travelling.

  • Carry previous prescriptions and relevant reports
  • Keep a current list of medicines and allergies
  • Note your symptoms and questions in advance
  • Confirm appointment time, location and instructions
